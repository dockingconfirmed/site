4h of building + 1h of troubleshooting

Today, we assembled the frame, estimated the wire extension lengths for the motors, soldered the wire extensions, then attempted to solder them all to the ESC board. Again, we had difficulty soldering to the solder pads and we ran out of solder wires.

After that, we did a little bit of troubleshooting. We tested the battery again with the charger and, for some reason, it's only detecting 1 cell instead of 3. We may need to buy another battery if the one we have is really defective. But we'll look for other ways to confirm it.

Lastly, we fixed the stuck filament at the lab's 3D printer and was able to start drying the TPU. So we might be able to print and test the mounts tomorrow.
