~5h of building

More flight tests were completed.

First thing we did for the day was to setup the ESP32 DroneBridge telemetry so we could get live logs on Mission Planner while flying. After that, we went straight into another flight test. During this flight test, we noted some behaviors that needed some fixing such as the drone drifting in position as well as lowering down even in altitude hold mode.

We learned that the vibration levels of the drone, or the VIBE parameter in mission planner, ranged from 5-15 m/s<sup>2</sup>, which was well under the acceptable value of 30 m/s<sup>2</sup>, so that was good. We also were able to filter out the motor noise for better gyro readings using harmonic notch filtering. The peaks were at 55-67 Hz so setting the INS_HNTCH_FREQ parameter to 58 filtered the motor noise out from the gyro readings. We also widened the altitude hold band so there would be more room for the stick to be in hover mode.

After those fixes, altitude hold worked perfectly, maintaining a 5.67 m altitude with ±5 cm error for about 20 seconds. The position drift was never fixed because we needed the compass to work and we have reason to believe it's defective. Although there might be a way to do position hold with just the GPS and the gyro that we were not aware of earlier.

Next, we attempted tune the PID controller using the AutoTune feature. We can only do one axis per flight because of the undersized battery. We chose the roll axis for the attempt. Unfortunately, the attempt failed because the battery ran out of charge. The position drift meant we had to constantly interrupt the AutoTune process which consumed power and occasionally reset the progress bar.

And that is probably where Raven 0 ends. We learned a lot. We went from literally zero knowledge about drones to building one ourselves and successfully performing flight tests in just about a month. The first Raven did its job well. Even though we weren't able to smooth out the rough edges, it's time to move on and carry on the learnings to Raven 1. It will feature a camera for relative positioning as well as the docking mechanism, the main attraction for Project Raven.
