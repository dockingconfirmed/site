3h of building

I soldered the motors (barely) to the ESC board and tested all of them successfully in Mission Planner. It didn't go very smoothly though. The motors wouldn't run at first because of a loose connection from the FC to the ESC plus shorted power pins that resulted from fixing the loose connection. Thankfully, we we're testing with a power supply (with OCP) instead of a battery because the boards could have been cooked due to the short. We lost a lot of time because we tried messing with the configurations in Mission Planner which obviously got us nowhere since it was a hardware issue.

Anyhow, we still got the motors to run and we tested it up to 15% throttle at 11.1 V simultaneously, consuming about 1.1 A of current from the power supply.

Tom was also able to successfully connect the RC receiver to the FC yesterday so the transmitter inputs have been tested and calibrated in Mission Planner. But we weren't able to arm the system and control the motors using the RC transmitter yet.

Next up, we'll test the motors with RC, test the ESP32 telemetry bridge, and plan the complete layout of components on the frame in preparation for flight testing next week.
