2h:50m of building

We successfully flashed ArduPilot firmware into the 9iMOD flight controller (Betaflight worked out-of-the-box but we couldn't flash the ArduPilot firmware using the Betaflight Configurator so we used dfu-util).

The accelerometer, gyroscope, and barometer all worked well and produced accurate altitude and attitude readings. We were surprised that the FC was able to accurately measure relative yaw angle as well with just the gyroscope.

The GPS module was not detected. It was receiving power but the LED power indicator was faint and flickering so the USB connection might not have enough current. That also made us defer testing the RC receiver.

The layout for the components on the frame was also planned. I will be be designing mounts for the ESP32, GPS module, and the RC receiver, and then 3D print it next session.

Next session, we plan on doing tests using a power supply. Hopefully the GPS module powers up.
