Bought a AA battery pack as a temporary battery for the FS-i6X. Planning on getting rechargeable AA batteries.

Binded FS-iA6B receiver and transmitter. I hooked up a 5V power supply to channel 2 (could hook to any channel).  More info on receiver:
- hook to VCC a power supply of 4.0-8.4 V DC
- red LED blinking = attempting to bind
- red LED ON = bounded and connected to transmitter
- that loop cord thing included with the receiver is used for binding the receiver to the transmitter --> insert to B
- to enter bind mode, hold the bind key button while turning the transmitter on; wait for the 'done' sound

Set up a Raven mode with type airplane / glider as recommended in the internet since there is no quadcopter mode --> allows mixing of the 6 channels.
